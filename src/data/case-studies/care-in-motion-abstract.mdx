### The Challenge

An effort to re-humanize the infrastructure of healthcare — to design for dignity and presence, to make space for recovery, storytelling, and connection.

As Margaret Mead once observed, the first sign of civilization was not a tool or technology, but a healed femur — evidence that someone had been cared for long enough to recover.

Care in Motion draws from that insight: a truly civilized healthcare system is one that prioritizes care, community, and human continuity.
